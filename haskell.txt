ls -t causes the files to be listed in "time" order:
the order in which they were last changed, most recent first.

% ls -lt
total 80
-rw-r--r--  1 home  staff     3 Oct  2 15:09 temp
-rw-r--r--  1 home  staff    16 Oct  2 15:04 junk2
-rw-r--r--  1 home  staff    10 Oct  2 14:55 junk

"total 80" tells how many blocks of disc space the files occupy; a block is usually either 512 or 1024 characters.

The fifth form (-c) deletes (clears) all xattrs.
